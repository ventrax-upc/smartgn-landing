What do we do?

We digitalize traditional natural gas management through advanced IoT infrastructure. We connect your physical meters to a highly scalable cloud platform, capable of processing consumption telemetry, pressure, and valve states in real-time.

How do we solve your problem?

We eliminate uncertainty in your bills and slow reaction times to incidents. You will receive immediate alerts via email, SMS, or WhatsApp regarding any anomaly or potential leak, and you'll be able to view your expenses in PEN before the end of the month.

Why trust us?

SmartGN is an ecosystem developed by Ventrax, founded on clean architectures, end-to-end data encryption, and high availability (99.5%). Additionally, we integrate a centralized directory of verified technicians ready to assist you.
